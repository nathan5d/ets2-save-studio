Safety & backups

Your progress deserves a safety net.

ETS2 Save Studio is designed to reduce risk before, during and after a supported edit.

  • The previous game.sii is backed up before a successful edit.
  • Writes use temporary files and post-write validation.
  • External changes are detected before a money edit is committed.
  • Restore preserves the current save before replacing it.

Important: safeguards reduce risk but cannot prevent every hardware failure, mod conflict or future game change. Keep independent copies of important saves.

How it works

Three deliberate steps.

Prepare a compatible local save, make a focused change and let the app protect the previous state.

Prepare your save

Disable Steam Cloud in Steam and in the ETS2 profile. Use the app to prepare new text-format saves.

Select and review

Close ETS2 completely, refresh the app and select the local profile and manual save you want to edit.

Apply with backup

Review the detected values, apply the change and keep the automatic backup available for restoration.
